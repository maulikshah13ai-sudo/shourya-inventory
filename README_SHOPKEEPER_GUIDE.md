# 👜👞 Bags & Shoes Wholesale + Retail Inventory System
### Easy Step-by-Step User Guide (No Coding Required!)

Welcome! This web application was built specifically for your wholesale and retail bags and footwear business.

---

## 🚀 Step 1: How to Start the App (1-Click Launch)

1. Open this folder:
   ```
   C:\Users\Admin\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\bag-shoe-inventory
   ```
2. Double-click **`Start_Shop_App.bat`**.
3. A black window will open and automatically open your web browser to:
   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:5000
   ```
4. **Desktop Shortcut:** Double-click **`Create_Desktop_Shortcut.bat`** once. A desktop icon named **"Shop Inventory System"** will appear on your Windows desktop so you can start it anytime!
5. **To close:** Simply close the black window when you are done for the day.

---

## 📋 Step 2: How to Use the App Every Day

### 1. ⚡ Fast Billing & POS (Stock Out)
* Click **"New Bill / POS"** from the top menu.
* **Choose Billing Mode:**
  * **Retail Mode:** Click this when selling 1 or 2 items to walk-in customers at retail MRP price.
  * **Wholesale Mode:** Click this when selling bulk quantities to shop owners or dealers. The system will automatically use the wholesale price and allow you to select the wholesale buyer.
* **Adding Items:** Type the model name, size (e.g. `UK 8` or `35L`), or SKU in the search box, or just click on any bag/shoe card on the screen.
* Enter quantity and discount if any.
* Choose payment method: **Cash**, **UPI / QR Code**, **Card**, or **Credit / Khata** (Pay later).
* Click **"COMPLETE SALE & PRINT BILL"**:
  * Your physical stock automatically decreases.
  * A clean printable bill/tax invoice opens ready to print or save as PDF.

---

### 2. 🚚 Stock In (When New Shipments Arrive from Factories)
* When you receive bulk cartons from manufacturers:
* Click **"Stock In (Purchases)"** in the top menu.
* Enter the Supplier name (e.g. *Apex Factory Distributors*) and their Challan/Invoice number.
* Pick the bag or shoe items and how many units/pairs arrived (e.g., 50 pairs of sneakers).
* Enter the unit cost price.
* Click **"RECEIVE & UPDATE INVENTORY"**.
* Your stock counts and inventory investment value update instantly!

---

### 3. 📦 Products & Stock Management
* Click **"Products & Stock"** to see all items.
* Filter by **Bags** 🎒 or **Shoes** 👞.
* Watch for **Low Stock Alert** warnings so you never run out of popular shoe sizes or bag models.
* Use the **Quick Adjust (+/-)** button to correct stock if items are damaged or found.
* Click **"+ Add New Product"** anytime you launch a new style or model.

---

### 4. 👥 Wholesale Khata (Credit Ledger)
* If dealers buy on credit (Udhar):
* Go to **"Wholesale Khata"** to see who owes you money.
* When a buyer pays you via Cash or UPI, click **"Receive Money"**, type the amount, and click Save. The pending balance will update immediately!

---

### 5. ⚙️ Customize Your Shop Details
* Go to **"Settings"** (gear icon in the top right).
* Enter your exact Shop Name, Address, Phone Number, and GST Number.
* Change your currency symbol (₹, $, etc.) and invoice return policy.
* These details will appear neatly at the top of every printed bill!

---

## 💾 Where is Your Data Stored?
* All data is saved inside a single database file: **`inventory.db`** inside this folder.
* **No cloud subscription or internet connection required!** It runs 100% locally on your PC.
* **Backup:** Simply copy the `inventory.db` file to a USB thumb drive once a week for safe backup.
